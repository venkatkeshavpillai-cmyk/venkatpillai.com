About

Finance across borders, from the inside

I am Group CFO of SLK Global, part of West Ireland Investments. The group runs nine entities across the UAE, India, Poland, Romania, Singapore, Qatar and the USA, in energy, procurement and sub-contracting.

My work sits where margins usually leak: foreign exchange, tax and transfer pricing, cash across jurisdictions, and the controls that hold a multi-country group together.

Chartered Accountant in India (ICAI) and England & Wales (ICAEW). Member, FICCI CFO Council. Based in Kochi.

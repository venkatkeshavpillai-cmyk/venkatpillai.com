About

Finance across borders, from the inside

I am Venkat Keshav Pillai, known professionally as Venkat Pillai, Group CFO of SLK Global, part of West Ireland Investments. The group runs nine entities across the UAE, India, Poland, Romania, Singapore, Qatar and the USA, in energy, procurement and sub-contracting. Each month I run the close and group consolidation across seven currencies, under IFRS, Ind AS and US GAAP.

My work sits where margins usually leak: foreign exchange, tax and transfer pricing, cash across jurisdictions, and the controls that hold a multi-country group together.

Chartered Accountant (ICAI and ICAEW), CPA (CPA Australia), FCMA and CGMA (CIMA), and Certified Fraud Examiner (ACFE). Member, FICCI CFO Council. Based between Dubai, Bengaluru and Kochi.
